# 📺 VideoCaster

> Seamlessly cast your favorite videos from your iOS device directly to any Chromecast-supported screen!

VideoCaster bridges the gap between your device and your TV. Whether you are casting local videos stored in your Photo Gallery or streaming media directly from your Telegram chats, VideoCaster handles it effortlessly—even in the background.

---

## ✨ Features

* **Multi-Source Casting:** Cast videos directly from your iOS Photo Gallery or straight from your **Telegram** chats.
* **Background Playback:** Runs smoothly in the background. You don’t need to keep the app open or leave your phone unlocked while streaming.
* **OS Media Control:** Control playback (play, pause, skip) directly through the native iOS system media controller from your Lock Screen or Control Center.

## 🛠️ Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you meet the following requirements:

* ⚠️ **Network:** Your iOS device and your Chromecast device **must be connected to the exact same Wi-Fi network**.
* 🔑 **Telegram Account:** A valid Telegram account is required to authenticate and stream videos directly from your chats.

## ⚙️ Installation

**1. Clone the repository**
```bash
git clone https://github.com/HarelZadok/VideoCaster.git
```

**2. Install Dependencies**  
*Open the project in Xcode. If you are using Swift Package Manager, dependencies will resolve automatically.*

**3. Build and Run**  
* Select your target iOS device.
* Select a build scheme:
  * **VideoCaster** - debug build
  * **VideoCaster-ReleaseBuild** - release build
* Run the app.

## 📱 How to Use

1. **Network Check:** Verify both your iOS device and Chromecast are on the same Wi-Fi network.
2. **Telegram Authentication:** On the first launch, securely connect the app to your Telegram account. This grants the app permission to read and pull video media from your chats.
3. **Select & Cast:** Choose a video from your local gallery or navigate through your Telegram media.
4. **Enjoy:** Tap the cast icon to connect to your Chromecast device. You can now lock your phone and control the playback right from your Lock Screen!
